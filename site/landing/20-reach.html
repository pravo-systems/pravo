Why it matters

Reach

Reads follow the change

A plan starts at your change and follows the recorded dependencies. It reads what the change reaches, and the apply locks and writes only that. The requests it sends to your cloud depend on the change, not on the size of the estate. Drift in a resource it does not reach is found by pravo drift or by a plan that does.

What a plan reaches Refresh age

A budget for staleness

Each record remembers when it was last read from the cloud. The refresh age sets how stale it may be before a plan reads it again, and a scheduled pravo drift --due reads the most overdue records, up to a limit you set.

Split by ownership, not by size

An estate can grow without its requests to the cloud growing with it, so there is no need to split a state to keep it fast. Split it where ownership, permissions or blast radius divide. Applies to different resources run at the same time; an overlapping apply is refused, not queued.
