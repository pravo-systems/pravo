Why I built this.

I'm Marcin Wyszynski. I co-founded Spacelift and OpenTofu, and I had been exploring CUE for a while. Ten years of running Terraform and OpenTofu left me with notes on what I would do differently, and one day I decided to throw some tokens at the problem and see what gives.

pravo is the result. The notes are mine, and the code was written with Claude from them. It is a study: it drives real OpenTofu providers and its behaviour is documented and tested, but nobody runs it for real yet. It is a non-commercial side project, not a product or a company, released under Apache 2.0 for anyone to use, change and build on. Take it for a spin and tell me what you found.
