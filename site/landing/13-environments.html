Environments

One specification. Every environment.

dev, staging and prod are declared in the specification, and a row for each holds what differs. The resources are written once and never name an environment. A release runs in dev first, and prod takes only what dev has run. Each environment keeps a state of its own and its own list of who may change it.

01 · The rows

Declared once, checked for all

The rows of $environments hold what differs. pravo validate checks the specification in every environment before a merge, so a row that misses a field is caught there and not in a plan.

 02 · The promotion flow

Prod takes what dev ran

$after says which environment an environment follows. Every apply leaves a signed note of the release it ran, and pravo promote checks it before the next environment takes the release. An admin of prod is refused too: the role decides who may promote, and the promotion decides what prod takes.

 03 · The states

Separate states, separate people

Each environment has a state of its own and its own list of readers, so a writer of dev can be a reader of prod. --env prod selects both.
