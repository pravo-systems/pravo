pravo serve, style guide

What the viewer is made of. Every value on this page is read from assets/tokens.css and the components are the real ones from app.css: change a token and this page changes with the viewer.

Principles

Square corners, hairline borders, flat sheets. Depth comes from a line and a tint, not from a shadow. Shadows are for what floats: menus, the palette, tooltips.
Rounded corners, a coloured stripe on one side of a box, a glow, a gradient wash over a card.
Colour means one thing: green adds or is valid, amber changes, red conflicts or destroys, blue is structure or protection, magenta is an overlap of plans. Nothing else is coloured.
Decorative colour, a second meaning for a colour, status told by colour alone (add a word, a glyph or a position).
The graph is a data flow: sources left, consumers right, wires orthogonal, arrowheads only right or down.
Diagonal wires, arrows that point left, repeated controls, a label nobody explained (give it an (i)).

Colour

Two themes, paper and ink, from the same names. Always use the name, never the value.

surfaces and text
meaning: each colour with its wash (the tint used behind it)

Type

Plex Sans for words, Plex Mono for what is typed or measured (addresses, types, versions, numbers), Newsreader for the title of a page and nothing smaller. Five small steps; anything else is a display size set on its own.

Shape and line

One border weight (1px), two strengths: --line inside a sheet, --line-strong around one. Selection is a wash and a full outline, never a stripe on one edge.

Motion

Motion explains a change; it never decorates. Short (140 ms) for a state, medium (420 ms) for something that moves, --ease (an ease-out) for everything that arrives, linear only for a progress that has a length. All of it stops under prefers-reduced-motion and motion=off.

Icons

Drawn as masks, so they take the colour of the text. 1.3 to 2.4 px strokes, square ends, no fills except a mark.
